About Bongshai Steel

Pioneering Steel Construction in Bangladesh

Bongshai Steel is a premier steel building manufacturer and EPC contractor based in Uttara, Dhaka. We engineer durable, earthquake-resistant, pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB) and modular steel structures.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing factory delivers high precision steel beams, C/Z purlins, decking sheets, and insulated sandwich panels designed for 50+ year lifespans under BNBC building regulations.

Turnkey Solutions

Our Specialized Services

Architectural & Structural Design

Complete 3D CAD modeling, Tekla structural detailing, and BNBC seismic load calculations by expert structural engineers.

Precision Steel Fabrication

Automated CNC cutting, SAW welding, surface sandblasting, and epoxy anti-corrosion coating in our Uttara factory.

On-Site Erection & Installation

Experienced site erection crews, heavy crane mobilization, high-strength bolt tightening, and safety supervision.

Zero Accident Commitment

Health And Safety Policy (EHS)

Bongshai Steel maintains strict Environmental, Health, and Safety (EHS) policies across all factory floors and construction erection sites nationwide.

  • Mandatory PPE compliance (hard hats, safety harnesses, steel-toe boots).
  • ISO 9001, 14001, and OHSAS 18001 safety protocol compliance.
  • 100% certified crane operators and certified high-altitude welders.
  • Regular site hazard audits and emergency response protocols.
